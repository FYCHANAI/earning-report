I. Our Views

Updated: 2026/10/09. Based on unaudited Q2 2026 results for the period ended 2026/06/30, released on 2026/09/01, plus Q3 delivery actuals announced on 10/01 and the 09/27 energy cooperation agreements. Q3 financial figures remain guidance or market forecasts.

🎯

Core Operational Conclusions

  • 1.
    Revenue Grows 69.1%, but Earnings Quality Still Needs Testing

    Q2 revenue reached RMB32.1369bn, up 69.1% YoY and 25.9% QoQ. Deliveries were 107,658, up 49.4% YoY. Vehicle margin was 18.5%, up 8.2 percentage points YoY but down 0.3 points from Q1. GAAP operating loss was RMB347.2m and net loss RMB528.0m. Excluding share-based compensation, adjusted operating profit was RMB206.9m and net profit RMB26.1m, leaving an adjusted net margin of only about 0.08%. Deliveries and revenue fell 2.1% and 2.0% below the respective lower ends of earlier guidance. We believe the annual improvement is clear, but the thin earnings cushion and sequential margin decline mean growth has yet to produce stable GAAP profitability.

  • 2.
    Q3 Deliveries Meet Guidance; Revenue and Earnings Remain Unreported

    The 10/01 update reported Q3 deliveries of 109,178, up 25.4% YoY and 1.4% QoQ, within the earlier 108,000–111,000 guidance. September deliveries were 37,408 and nine-month deliveries totaled 300,301. Q3 revenue guidance issued on 09/01 was RMB33.285–34.051bn; Bloomberg EEO consensus is RMB33.053bn with Adj+ EPS of RMB0.020. These financial figures are not reported actuals. We believe delivery mix, discounts and expense discipline will determine whether meeting volume guidance translates into earnings. Source: delivery update

  • 3.
    A Larger Cash and Investment Balance; Energy Cooperation Awaits Execution

    At 06/30, cash, cash equivalents, restricted cash, short-term investments and long-term time deposits totaled RMB56.7bn; cash and cash equivalents alone were RMB17.4536bn. These measures are distinct. The company confirmed positive Q2 operating cash flow without a verified quarterly cash-flow or capex amount, so free cash flow cannot be calculated from the available disclosure. Agreements signed with Geely on 09/27 provide for RMB640m cash and all equity in Yiyi Internet in exchange for 30% of NIO Power at a RMB16bn post-money valuation, subject to regulatory and closing conditions. We believe network integration may share investment costs, but that valuation is not the listed group's equity value and the consideration is not cash received in Q2. Source: agreements

  • 4.
    At 33.05x FY2027 P/E, Valuation Depends on Delivering Earnings

    Bloomberg EEO FY2027 Adj+ P/E is 8.23x for Leapmotor, 11.69x for BYD, 33.05x for NIO and 37.92x for Li Auto. NIO's next-four-quarter Adj+ P/E is 94.76x, a different period that highlights sensitivity to earnings timing and the forecast denominator. The 10/09 ANR consensus target of US$6.23 implies 82.7% upside from the displayed 10/08 reference price of US$3.41, excluding dividends. We believe that potential return must be assessed against durable earnings and cash generation; differences in business models and accounting adjustments mean a lower multiple does not establish lower risk.

V. Risk Factors

  1. Price Competition and a Thin Earnings Cushion: Q2 vehicle margin fell 0.3 percentage points sequentially, adjusted net profit was only RMB26.1m and the GAAP net loss remained RMB528.0m. Deeper discounts, a weaker product mix or higher battery and component costs could turn the small adjusted profit back into a loss.
  2. Multi-Brand Deliveries and Expense Execution: Q2 deliveries and revenue missed the lower ends of earlier guidance. Q3 deliveries met guidance but grew only 1.4% sequentially. New launches, supply-chain ramp-ups and distribution investment may add costs; Q2 selling, general and administrative expenses grew 26.5% QoQ, faster than revenue at 25.9%.
  3. Liquidity and Energy Transaction Execution: The RMB56.7bn cash and investment balance includes restricted funds and is not entirely available for unrestricted use; positive operating cash flow does not establish positive cash flow after capex. The Geely transaction remains subject to regulatory and closing conditions, while integration, investment and ownership-adjustment provisions may affect the benefits.
  4. Forecast Valuation, ADS and Policy Risks: FY2027 P/E relies on future earnings and does not guarantee realized returns. ADSs trade in USD while underlying financial figures are in RMB, exposing prices to currency and cross-border regulatory changes. EV policy, product safety and intelligent-driving requirements may also increase costs.
