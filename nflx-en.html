I. Our Views

🎯

Core Operational Conclusions

  • 1.
    Results In Line with Guidance; Full-Year Profit Path Intact

    FQ2 revenue of $12.56 billion (+13.4% YoY, +12% FX-neutral) was essentially in line with the company's forecast, while the 33.4% operating margin and diluted EPS of $0.80 (+11.1%) came in above the company's forecasts of 32.6% and $0.78, respectively. Full-year revenue guidance was narrowed to $51.0–51.4 billion (+13%–14%) with the 31.5% operating margin guidance maintained, implying 20%+ full-year operating income growth.

  • 2.
    Advertising & Live Form the Second Growth Engine

    The 2026 advertising revenue target of roughly $3 billion (about double 2025) is backed by US upfront negotiations entering their final stage; live content accounts for only ~5% of content spend and ~1% of view hours, yet delivered six of the top ten member-addition days of the past five years, with expanded NFL rights, the Women's World Cup, MLB and WWE set to extend this acquisition lever.

  • 3.
    Content Engine & GenAI Efficiency Advancing in Tandem

    First-half viewing exceeded 97 billion hours (+2%) with non-English content over one-third of the mix; GenAI workflows have been applied to roughly 300 titles, and the 17-minute AI-enhanced segment in American Experiment was completed at half the cost and twice the speed. Full-year content amortization is set to grow only ~10%, demonstrating investment discipline.

  • 4.
    Valuation Re-Rating Potential Amid Accelerated Buybacks

    FQ2 buybacks hit a single-quarter record of $4.7 billion with $27.1 billion of authorization remaining, and full-year free cash flow guidance stands at roughly $12.5 billion. In our view, after a 41.6% share-price decline over the past year, the 21.5x forward P/E is attractive relative to double-digit revenue and 20%+ operating income growth guidance. The ANR consensus target of $107.85 implies roughly 45.1% upside based on the July 16, 2026 closing price of $74.35.

IV. Analyst Views

Summary of top Wall Street investment banks' latest takes following Netflix's FQ2 2026 results (ratings and price targets: Bloomberg ANR, as of 07/17/2026):

Morgan Stanley

Overweight
  • Overweight Maintained (PT $83): Analyst Sean Diffley maintained his Overweight rating (industry view In-Line) after the July 17 results; based on the July 16 close of $74.35, the target implies ~12% upside — below the $107.85 ANR consensus mean, marking a relatively cautious bull.
  • Focus on the Pricing Cycle & GenAI Cost Savings: His questions on the call centered on subscriber acceptance and timing of the current price increases, and on GenAI content-cost savings following the InterPositive deal; management noted first-half increases in the US, Mexico and Spain are tracking in line with past cycles, with savings to be reinvested in content.

Goldman Sachs

Buy
  • Buy Maintained (PT $94): Analyst Eric Sheridan maintained his Buy rating after the July 17 results; the target implies ~26% upside and sits in the lower-middle of the ANR buy-rated target range (~$83–151.4).
  • A Microcosm of the Positive Sell-Side Consensus: With the stock down 41.6% over the past year, buy ratings still account for 79.7% of the ANR sample with no sell ratings; Goldman's stance aligns with this positive skew, though its target sits ~13% below the consensus mean — optimism with a note of caution.

UBS

Buy
  • Buy Maintained (PT $115): Analyst John Hodulik updated on July 16, results day, with a target above the $107.85 ANR consensus mean implying ~55% upside — among the more constructive in this coverage sample.
  • Focus on the Long-Term Roadmap for New Content Formats: His questions centered on the expansion path and success metrics for video podcasts, vertical video, games and other emerging formats; management responded that podcasts are clearly additive to daytime and mobile viewing, framing the broader format expansion as "evolution, not revolution."

V. Risk Factors

  1. Uncertainty Around Content-Spend Intensity & New-Business Returns: 2026 content amortization is expected to grow ~10% (above the ~8% five-year average) with cash content spend at ~1.1x amortization; live, games and podcasts remain early-stage investments, and slower-than-expected payback could compress medium-term margin expansion.
  2. FX & International Market Volatility: Markets outside the US and Canada contribute ~57% of revenue; FQ2 FX-neutral growth (+12%) trailed reported growth (+13.4%), indicating a currency tailwind this quarter. The 31.5% full-year margin guidance is based on January 1, 2026 FX rates, so a stronger dollar would pressure reported results.
  3. Slowing Engagement Growth & Reduced Disclosure Frequency: First-half view hours grew 2%, and the company will move the What We Watched report to an annual Q1 release from 2027; the market may read the lower cadence as reduced engagement transparency, and scrutiny of returning-season viewership and long-term retention may persist.
  4. Execution Risk on the Advertising Target: The ~$3 billion full-year ad revenue goal (roughly double 2025) hinges on US upfront negotiations closing within weeks and continued scaling of the in-house ad tech stack and programmatic channels; a macro ad-budget pullback or underperforming live content could delay the second growth curve.
