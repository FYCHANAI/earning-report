## Essential earnings-quality context

1. Source 3 p2: Q3 2025 recognized ~$619m Brazilian non-income-tax-dispute expense as cost of revenue, >5pp adverse operating margin. Most pertains to 2022–2024, ~20% to 2025. Hence Q3 2026 +37.3% OP and +6.4pp margins benefit from low comparison base. Analytical add-back: prior OP 3248.247+619=3867.247m; margin33.5981%; Q3 2026 OP growth vs this base15.2913%, margin uplift1.0340pp. These adjustments are our analysis, NOT company adjusted Q3 2026 results. Keeping prose qualitative is safest if no separate adjusted metric is needed.
2. Source 4 pp2,5: Q1 2026 recognized and received $2.8bn Warner-related termination fee in interest/other income and OCF. Company raised FY FCF to12.5bn primarily its after-tax impact. Source 2 p6 says Q2 cash taxes included fee-related taxes. Thus FY2026 FCF current $12bn and nine-month EPS/FCF include nonrecurring fee effects; avoid equating all annual FCF to recurring streaming generation or assuming no fee because deal not proceeding.
3. Current Q3 margin beat chiefly expense timing. Do not extrapolate 34.6% quarterly margin as steady-state, especially Q4 guide26% with restructuring.

## Catalysts/risk evidence and suggested client-facing thesis

Source 1 pp3-5: 2027 ad-tier expansion to15 new countries; initial March tranche includes nine EMEA plus Colombia/Peru/New Zealand (12 countries in first tranche). Programmatic access/measurement work progressing. Q4 largest live slate to date, including three NFL games and Fury/Joshua. India Q3 viewing +24%, constant-currency revenue~18%, record quarterly paid net adds. Non-English now >1/3 viewing vs <10% decade ago. Do not equate audience approaching1bn with paid memberships.

Possible analytical framing (our opinions, not bank views):
- Subscription retention/pricing remains resilient; revenue +11.8% confirms growth but deceleration and ad forecast cut reduce near-term multiple expansion catalyst.
- Margin expansion continues but headline improvement overstates underlying acceleration because prior Brazil charge and current expense timing. FY31.5% target holding is more useful than extrapolating Q3 peak.
- Strong cash generation and buybacks support per-share earnings; current repurchases exceed quarterly FCF and FY FCF includes deal fee, so pace/sustainability matters.
- Re-rating needs ad-product delivery and engagement acceleration; 2027 geography expansion and Q4 live programming offer catalysts, with execution/content-cost/competition risk. Valuation wording must incorporate parent's verified Bloomberg facts.
