I. Our Views

Updated: 2026/10/22. Quarter ended 2026/09/30; results released 2026/10/21. Market-data dates are identified separately.

🎯

Core Operational Conclusions

  • 1.
    Gross-margin recovery precedes operating leverage

    Q3 revenue was broadly flat year on year at US$28.046bn. Automotive margin excluding regulatory credits recovered from 16.3% to 18.1% sequentially, but operating expenses rose 39% year on year and operating margin was only 2.6%. We believe product mix and software revenue are beginning to support automotive profitability; sustained earnings expansion requires revenue growth to absorb research and share-based compensation costs.

  • 2.
    FSD broadens monetization; Robotaxi remains early-stage

    Active FSD customers reached 1.74m (upfront purchases and subscriptions, excluding free trials) and the North American new-delivery attach rate exceeded 75%. Cybercab joined the Austin Robotaxi fleet in September. These developments broaden software and fleet revenue opportunities. We believe rerating depends on paid-customer retention, service coverage and unit economics rather than mileage or fleet size alone. FSD (Supervised) still requires active driver supervision.

  • 3.
    Strong storage demand has yet to translate fully into revenue

    Storage deployments rose approximately 10% year on year to 13.7 GWh, while energy revenue fell 16% to US$2.881bn, partly reflecting tariff-related customer refunds. Production began at Megafactory Texas, adding future supply capability. We believe profitable conversion of new capacity into deliveries and a closer alignment between revenue and deployments are the key catalysts; deployment growth alone can overstate near-term revenue momentum.

  • 4.
    Positive free cash flow, but valuation demands stronger execution

    Operating cash flow of US$6.263bn less US$4.732bn of capex produced US$1.531bn of free cash flow; increases in payables, accruals and deferred revenue also supported cash generation. FY2027 estimated P/E is 174.01x, while the US$407.05 ANR target implies 5.3% upside from US$386.47. We believe liquidity supports long-term investment, but the valuation still sets a high bar for new-business success, capital efficiency and earnings conversion.

V. Risk Factors

  1. Autonomy and robotics monetization: Robotaxi and Optimus must establish reliability, safety records and scaled commercial deployment. Regulatory restrictions, accidents or weaker adoption may delay software and fleet profitability.
  2. Automotive demand and price competition: Deliveries declined year on year, while average selling prices and regulatory-credit revenue remained under pressure. Intensifying competition or adverse product mix could interrupt margin recovery.
  3. Capital spending and working capital: Q3 capex was US$4.732bn; higher payables, accruals and deferred revenue supported cash generation. Reversals in payment timing or slower project returns could pressure free cash flow again.
  4. Valuation and energy volatility: The 174.01x FY2027 P/E is sensitive to delivery of future earnings. Storage refunds, tariffs, supply chains and production ramps can also create energy-revenue and profit volatility.
