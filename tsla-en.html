I. Our Views

🎯

Core Operational Conclusions

  • 1.
    Record Second-Quarter Deliveries; TTM Revenue Tops $100B for the First Time

    Q2 total deliveries reached 480,126 units (+25% YoY, a record for any second quarter), with total revenues of $28.24B (+26% YoY); trailing-twelve-month revenue exceeded $100B for the first time. Energy storage deployments of 13.5 GWh marked the second-best quarter on record, and Services & Other gross profit hit a record $648M. We believe demand momentum across the core businesses has returned to a growth trajectory, with the company disclosing its largest order backlog since 2023.

  • 2.
    "Volume Up, Margin Down" — Profitability Under Structural Pressure

    Q2 operating income was $398M (−57% YoY) with an operating margin of just 1.4% (−269bp), pressured by lower vehicle ASPs, a sharp decline in regulatory credit revenue ($439M → $146M), a 47% rise in operating expenses (driven by AI and other R&D projects, stock-based compensation including the 2025 CEO Performance Award, and SG&A), and an approximately $240M vendor-cell warranty true-up in the Energy business. GAAP net income attributable to common stockholders was $1,114M, including an approximately $1,005M pre-tax unrealized fair-value gain on the SpaceX equity investment ($763M after-tax non-GAAP adjustment); we believe this non-operating, unrealized item should be considered separately when assessing core profitability.

  • 3.
    Largest Capex Cycle in Company History; Free Cash Flow Turns Negative

    Q2 capital expenditures reached $5.79B (+142% YoY, roughly doubling sequentially), turning free cash flow negative at −$1.09B. Management guides full-year capex above $25B with further growth over the next two to three years, and is arranging debt facilities of up to $30B to fund the Robotaxi fleet, Optimus capacity, semiconductor fabs and solar manufacturing. We believe the payback period of this investment cycle is long, making execution the key driver of valuation.

  • 4.
    FSD & Robotaxi Commercialization Accelerating, but Valuation Prices In High Optionality

    Active paid FSD customers reached 1.48 million (including upfront purchases and monthly subscriptions, excluding free trials; +56% YoY), with FSD attach rates on new North America deliveries above 55%. Robotaxi now covers seven major U.S. metros — the Bay Area still operates with safety drivers while the other six markets are ramping unsupervised — and Cybercab production has begun at Gigafactory Texas. Based on the 07/22/2026 close of US$374.01, the Bloomberg ANR consensus target of US$417.17 implies approximately 11.5% upside; however, at a forward P/E of roughly 177x — significantly above BYD and Li Auto and comparable to NIO — we believe the market has already priced in substantial long-term optionality from autonomy and robotics. The Bloomberg ANR consensus rating is "Hold".

IV. Analyst Views

(Ratings & price targets: Bloomberg ANR, as of 07/23/2026)

Morgan Stanley

Equal-weight
  • Equal-weight (PT US$400): Focus on the pace of Robotaxi expansion across seven U.S. metros and the Cybercab production ramp; the speed of autonomy commercialization remains the core long-term value driver.
  • Cash-flow pressure during the investment phase: Doubling capex turned quarterly free cash flow negative; with debt facilities of up to $30B being arranged, execution and payback timing of the heavy-asset build-out are pivotal to the rating.

Goldman Sachs

Neutral
  • Neutral (PT US$360): Automotive gross margin excluding regulatory credits fell from 19.2% to 16.3% QoQ; lower ASPs and shrinking credit revenue limit near-term earnings visibility.
  • FSD penetration is the structural bright spot: Active paid FSD customers reached 1.48M (upfront and subscription, +56%) with North America attach rates above 55%, supporting a better long-term margin mix from software revenue.

UBS

Neutral
  • Neutral (PT US$385): Energy gross margin fell sharply from 39.5% to 20.4% on a warranty true-up and intensifying industrial-storage ASP competition, tempering the profit contribution of the second growth curve.
  • Valuation already reflects optimism: At a forward P/E of ~177x — significantly above BYD and Li Auto and comparable to NIO — continued delivery on Robotaxi and Optimus milestones is required to sustain the multiple.

V. Risk Factors

  1. Elevated Capex & Negative Free Cash Flow: Q2 capex doubled to $5.79B and free cash flow turned negative at −$1.09B; management guides full-year capex above $25B with growth over the next two to three years, alongside debt facilities of up to $30B — slower-than-expected ramps or returns would add financial strain.
  2. Persistent Pressure on Automotive Margins: Automotive gross margin excluding regulatory credits fell from 19.2% to 16.3% QoQ; regulatory credit revenue declined from $439M to $146M YoY, while lower ASPs, higher commodity costs and upfront recognition of interest-rate subvention weigh on near-term profitability.
  3. Energy Margin Volatility & Warranty Provisions: Energy gross margin fell from 39.5% to 20.4%, reflecting an approximately $240M vendor-cell warranty true-up, non-repeating tariff benefits from the prior quarter and rising industrial-storage ASP competition; management expects long-term margins to normalize in the low-to-mid 20% range.
  4. Autonomy Regulation & Supply-Chain Bottlenecks: Robotaxi expansion requires state-by-state regulatory clearance — management called New Jersey's state-level developments disheartening — while battery pack capacity and electronic components (including memory) remain the company's stated constraints on near-term production growth.
