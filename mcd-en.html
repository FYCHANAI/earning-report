I. Our Views

Updated: 2026/10/09. Based on Q2 2026 results for the period ended 2026/06/30, released on 2026/08/04, and the 10-Q, including the 09/23 NEXT strategy update. Company actuals, future targets and market consensus are identified separately.

Core Operational Conclusions

  • 1.
    Comparable Sales Grow, but US Traffic Has Yet to Recover

    Q2 revenue was US$7.099bn, up 4% reported and 2% in constant currencies. Global comparable sales grew 1.3%, versus 3.8% in the prior-year quarter. US growth of 0.8% reflected a higher average check and product mix, partly offset by negative comparable guest counts; International Operated Markets and International Developmental Licensed Markets grew 1.5% and 1.9%. Adjusted operating income was US$3.391bn, with a 47.8% margin. We believe pricing and mix can sustain sales, but durable growth requires a traffic recovery; positive comparable sales do not establish that guest counts have recovered.

  • 2.
    Franchising and Loyalty Support Cash Generation; Sales Definitions Matter

    Approximately 95% of restaurants are franchised. Q2 systemwide sales of about US$37bn cover franchised and company-operated restaurants and are not company revenue. Across 70 loyalty markets, trailing-twelve-month loyalty systemwide sales reached US$40bn, with nearly 220 million 90-day active users. Q2 operating cash flow was US$2.807bn; after US$0.831bn capex, free cash flow was US$1.976bn, up 57.6% YoY, versus US$3.706bn for the first half. We believe franchise and loyalty scale support cash generation, but loyalty sales must not be added to company revenue, nor does one quarter establish a full-year cash-flow trend.

  • 3.
    NEXT Targets Long-Term Efficiency with Investment and Franchisee Support

    The 09/23 NEXT update targets a corporate operating margin in the low-to-mid 50% range by 2030 and 250 basis points of gross restaurant-level efficiencies in the US and International Operated Markets upon full deployment. That is a restaurant-level gross target, not a guaranteed 250-basis-point increase in corporate operating margin. Baseline capex for 2027–2030 is expected to be about US$3bn annually, plus US$1.5–2.0bn of cumulative capital partnering support. We believe efficiency and digital initiatives create long-term potential, while franchisee investment, realized savings and rent-support costs require monitoring. Source: NEXT update.

  • 4.
    At 17.89x Forward P/E, Earnings Improvement Remains Essential

    Bloomberg EEO shows Q3 2026 consensus revenue of US$7.291bn and Adj+ EPS of US$3.403; these are market estimates, not company guidance. MCD trades at a displayed 17.89x next-four-quarter Adj+ P/E, above QSR at 16.47x and below YUM at 21.48x and SBUX at 31.30x. The 10/09 ANR consensus target of US$295.91 implies 24.9% upside from the displayed 10/08 reference price of US$236.90, excluding dividends. We believe traffic, franchisee returns and NEXT execution will determine valuation support; differences between peers and forecast risks cannot be assessed from the multiples alone.

V. Risk Factors

  1. Traffic, Consumer Affordability and Promotional Competition: US Q2 comparable sales grew 0.8%, but guest counts declined. Further pressure on dining budgets could prevent pricing and product mix from offsetting traffic losses, while deeper promotions could reduce restaurant margins. Growth in active loyalty users does not guarantee higher spending per visit or improved traffic across the system.
  2. NEXT Execution and Franchisee Investment Returns: The 2030 margin target depends on technology deployment, restaurant processes and franchisee cooperation. The 250-basis-point target represents gross restaurant-level efficiencies before all investment and support costs; additional capital partnering support of US$1.5–2.0bn over 2027–2030 is cumulative. Delays, cost overruns or inadequate franchisee returns may defer the benefits.
  3. Inflation, Labor and Restaurant Cost Pressures: Higher food, labor and occupancy costs may erode restaurant returns and weaken franchisees’ capacity to invest. Q2 adjusted operating margin was about 47.8%, slightly below approximately 47.9% a year earlier. Even with sales growth, overhead, organizational transformation and promotional spending may constrain operating leverage.
  4. Currency and Divergent International Markets: Q2 revenue rose 4% in reported currencies and 2% in constant currencies, highlighting the effect of currency translation on reported growth. Negative comparable sales in France within International Operated Markets and China within International Developmental Licensed Markets partly offset growth elsewhere. Regional demand, currencies and operating disruptions may widen performance differences.
