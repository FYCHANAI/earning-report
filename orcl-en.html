V. Risk Factors

  1. Intricate Sourcing and Advanced GPU Supply Concentration Risks: Operating complex cloud and hardware system pipelines across multiple tier-1 manufacturing boundaries leaves deployment cycles heavily vulnerable to chip and advanced component allocations.
  2. Hyperscale Datacenter Structural Capital Planning Capacity Pressures: Crucial dependence rests on the entity's baseline capacity to accurately schedule, negotiate, build out, and cool multi-region infrastructure facilities to sustain high data scale limits.
  3. Macro Volatility and International Tariff Frictions: Global deployments remain subject to changing cross-border administrative rulings, foreign exchange rate headwinds, trade policies, and unpredictable operational margins.
  4. Cybersecurity Threat Profiles and Strict Governance Legislation: Hyperscale computational platforms remain primary vectors for advanced persistent data breaches, configuration defects, or dynamic compliance liabilities that carry litigation or investigation overheads.
