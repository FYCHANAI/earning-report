I. Our Views

🎯

Core Operational Conclusions

  • 1.
    Broad Volume and Pricing Gains; Full-Year Guidance Raised

    Q2 net revenues grew 7% YoY to US$13.38 billion, with organic revenues (non-GAAP) up 6% (concentrate sales +4%, price/mix +2%); global unit case volume grew 5%, led by India, China, the United States and Brazil. The company raised full-year guidance: organic revenue growth of approximately 5%, comparable EPS (non-GAAP) growth of 9% to 10%, and free cash flow (non-GAAP) of approximately US$12.4 billion. We believe delivering volume-and-price growth alongside a guidance raise amid what management calls a dynamic consumer landscape underscores the resilience of the system's execution and brand portfolio.

  • 2.
    Quality Margin Expansion; Input Costs and Marketing Spend Bear Watching in H2

    Comparable operating margin (non-GAAP) reached 35.6%, up 86 bps YoY (GAAP 34.9%, +77 bps); underlying operating margin (non-GAAP), which excludes currency and acquisitions/divestitures, also expanded 24 bps, driven by organic revenue growth and lower operating expenses, partially offset by higher input costs and increased marketing investments (partly timing-related). We view this quarter's expansion as operationally driven rather than merely currency-assisted, though input cost pressure warrants continued monitoring.

  • 3.
    World Cup Activation and Innovation Engine Amplify Brand Momentum

    The FIFA World Cup campaign spanned more than 180 markets and generated over 60 billion digital and social impressions, contributing to Trademark Coca-Cola unit case volume growth of +5% and Powerade of +8%; Coca-Cola Zero Sugar volume grew 16%. The company is also establishing innovation hubs across operating units to lift and shift innovations across markets faster (e.g., Coca-Cola Zero Zero and the China-adapted Sprite+Tea). We believe this global-scale-with-local-execution marketing and innovation engine is a structural underpinning of its above-peer organic growth.

  • 4.
    Rich Valuation Premium; Moderate Upside

    Based on the closing price of US$88.27 on 07/28/2026, the stock offers approximately 5.0% upside to the Bloomberg ANR consensus target of US$92.64 (as of 07/29/2026). On valuation, FY2026 forward P/E stands at 26.88x, well above Nestlé at 18.48x, PepsiCo at 16.69x and KDP at 13.63x. We believe the premium reflects growth visibility and defensive characteristics; near-term share performance will hinge more on guidance delivery than further multiple expansion.

IV. Analyst Views

(Ratings and price targets: Bloomberg ANR, as of 07/29/2026)

Morgan Stanley

Overweight
  • Pricing Power and Share Momentum (PT US$100): Price/mix rose 2% this quarter, with continued value share gains across total NARTD and North America organic revenues (non-GAAP) up 7%. We believe the category leader's pricing and mix-upgrade capability underpins the fundamental case for an Overweight stance.
  • Guidance Raise Improves H2 Visibility: The company lifted full-year organic revenue guidance to approximately +5% and comparable EPS (non-GAAP) growth to +9% to +10%. We view guidance delivery as the key checkpoint for the rating and target trajectory ahead.

JP Morgan

Overweight
  • Emerging-Market Engine Restarts (PT US$96): Global unit case volume of +5% was led by India, China, the U.S. and Brazil, with Latin America organic revenues (non-GAAP) up 5% and Bottling Investments up 10%. We believe stabilizing emerging-market volumes are the key pivot supporting the full-year guidance raise.
  • Margin Resilience Under Cost Pressure: Comparable operating margin (non-GAAP) of 35.6% (+86 bps YoY) expanded despite higher input costs and increased marketing investments. We see this as evidence of expense leverage and revenue growth management execution.

UBS

Buy
  • Broad-Based, High-Quality Volume Growth (PT US$104, Highest of the Three): Coca-Cola Zero Sugar volume grew 16%, Powerade 8% and Trademark Coca-Cola 5%, with growth driven across categories and markets. We believe the breadth and quality of volume growth is the core logic behind the highest target of the three.
  • Cash Flow Raise Supports Shareholder Returns: Full-year free cash flow (non-GAAP) guidance was raised to approximately US$12.4 billion, with net debt leverage (non-GAAP) at 1.4x. We believe the balance sheet provides ample flexibility for dividends and reinvestment.

V. Risk Factors

  1. Consumer Environment and Health Preferences: Management continues to see a dynamic consumer landscape; obesity and other health-related concerns, evolving consumer product and shopping preferences, and perceived health consequences of sweeteners and other ingredients could weigh on long-term category demand.
  2. Input Costs and Supply Chain Pressure: Comparable operating margin (non-GAAP) expansion this quarter was partially offset by higher input costs; the company's risk disclosures also cite supply chain risks including increased commodity, raw material, packaging, energy and transportation costs, which could compress margins if pressures re-intensify.
  3. Currency Volatility: GAAP EPS growth this quarter included an approximately 4-point currency tailwind (comparable EPS approximately 2 points), and full-year guidance embeds an approximately 1% currency tailwind to comparable net revenues and approximately 3% to comparable EPS; a reversal in currency trends would directly erode translated growth.
  4. Tax Litigation and Transaction Execution: The tax dispute with the U.S. IRS remains ongoing, and the underlying effective tax rate (non-GAAP) of 19.9% does not include the impact if the company were not to prevail; the pending sale of bottling operations in Africa is subject to regulatory approvals, and closing timing will affect the magnitude of the acquisitions/divestitures headwind in guidance.
