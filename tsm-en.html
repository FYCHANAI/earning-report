I. Our Views

🎯

Core Operational Conclusions

  • 1.
    Revenue at the Top of Guidance; Gross and Operating Margins Beat the Ceiling

    2Q26 net revenue was NT$1,270.38B (US$40.20B, at the top of the US$39.0B–40.2B guidance range), up 12.0% QoQ and 36.0% YoY. Gross margin of 67.7% and operating margin of 60.3% both exceeded the guidance ceilings (65.5%–67.5% / 56.5%–58.5%). Net income attributable to shareholders reached NT$706.56B, up 77.4% YoY, with diluted EPS of NT$27.25. Management attributed the results to strong leading-edge demand, cost improvement, and higher capacity utilization.

  • 2.
    2nm Enters the Revenue Mix; Advanced Nodes and HPC Deepen Further

    2nm contributed 3% of wafer revenue for the first time this quarter, with 3nm/5nm at 30%/33% and advanced technologies (7nm and below) at 77%. By platform, HPC revenue grew 20% QoQ to 66% of net revenue. We believe the upgraded node and HPC mix supports medium- to long-term profitability, though the 3Q26 gross margin guidance midpoint of ~66% (below the 67.7% posted in 2Q26) points to near-term dilution from the steep N2 ramp and overseas fabs.

  • 3.
    Strong 3Q26 Guidance; FY2026 USD Revenue Growth of "Slightly Above 40%"

    Management guides 3Q26 revenue of US$44.6B–45.8B (midpoint ~US$45.2B, ~+12.4% QoQ), gross margin of 65%–67%, and operating margin of 56%–58% (assuming USD/NTD of 32), with 2nm entering a steep ramp; the company maintains its outlook for FY2026 USD revenue growth slightly above 40%.

  • 4.
    VIS Disposal and Mark-to-Market Gains Boost Non-Operating Income; Solid Finances Amid Heavy Investment

    Non-operating items were a NT$95.83B gain, including NT$63.20B of disposal and mark-to-market gains on Vanguard (VIS) shares — a non-core, volatility-prone source of earnings. 2Q26 capex was US$15.70B (US$26.80B YTD), free cash flow was NT$287.36B, and net cash stood at NT$2,486.33B, alongside a 1Q26 cash dividend of NT$7.00 per share (ex-dividend 09/16/2026).

IV. Analyst Views

Based on the 07/16/2026 closing price of $409.74, the ANR 12-month consensus price target of $624.59 implies 52.4% upside. Below is a summary of three banks' takes on TSMC's 2Q26 results (ratings & price targets: Bloomberg ANR, as of 07/17/2026):

Goldman Sachs

Buy
  • 2nm Volume Ramp Imminent (PT $620): 2nm contributed 3% of wafer revenue for the first time this quarter, and management guides a steep 3Q26 ramp; combined with HPC at 66% of revenue, leading-edge demand visibility extends into 2027.
  • Profitability Structurally Re-rated: Gross margin of 67.7% and operating margin of 60.3% both beat the guidance ceilings, as cost improvement and high utilization offset overseas fab dilution.

BNP Paribas

Overweight
  • Rising AI Demand Concentration (PT $526): HPC revenue grew 20% QoQ to 66% of the mix, and North America contributed 78% of revenue — evidence of deep customer reliance on advanced-node capacity.
  • Guidance Implies Undimmed 2H Momentum: 3Q26 revenue guidance of US$44.6B–45.8B (midpoint ~+12.4% QoQ) with a 65%–67% gross margin; management maintains FY2026 USD revenue growth slightly above 40%.

Barclays

Overweight
  • Resilient Cash Flow Despite Heavy Capex (PT $650): 1H26 capex reached US$26.80B (US$15.70B in 2Q26), while 2Q26 free cash flow was NT$287.36B and net cash NT$2,486.33B — balancing expansion and shareholder returns.
  • Core Earnings Require Stripping Non-Core Items: Non-operating income of NT$95.83B includes NT$63.20B of VIS disposal and mark-to-market gains; the effective tax rate rose to 18.1% on undistributed-earnings tax — both should be excluded when assessing core momentum.

V. Risk Factors

  1. FX Swings Erode Reported Revenue and Margins: 3Q26 guidance assumes USD/NTD of 32 (vs. a 2Q26 average of 31.60); NTD appreciation versus this assumption would directly compress NTD-reported revenue and margins.
  2. Margin Dilution from Overseas Fabs: Management noted that 2Q26 gross margin gains were partially offset by dilution from overseas fabs; as capacity in the US, Japan, and Europe ramps, overseas cost structures may weigh on long-term margins.
  3. Customer/Geographic Concentration and Geopolitics: North America accounted for 78% of 2Q26 revenue and HPC for 66%; the Safe Harbor statement also lists industry cyclicality, competition, order shifts from significant customers, FX volatility, and unexpected events, while trade and geopolitical developments could affect end demand.
  4. Heavy Capex and N2 Ramp Execution Risk: 1H26 capex reached US$26.80B, and inventory days rose 7 days QoQ to 87 on the N2 ramp; slower-than-expected advanced-node ramps or end demand would pressure earnings via depreciation and inventory digestion.
