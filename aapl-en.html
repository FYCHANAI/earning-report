I. Our Views

🎯

Core Operational Conclusions

  • 1.
    Strongest June Quarter Ever: Double-Digit Growth Across Three Major Categories and All Regions

    FQ3 total revenue rose 16.4% YoY to $109,417 M and diluted EPS climbed 28.7% YoY to $2.02, both June-quarter records; iPhone (+21.7%), Mac (+28.7%) and Services (+12.1%) revenue also set June-quarter records, with all five geographic segments delivering double-digit growth. Management called it the company's strongest June quarter ever.

  • 2.
    Gross Margin Tops 50%, but the Tariff-Refund Benefit Must Be Isolated

    Gross margin of 50.1% (up 3.6 ppts YoY) includes a favorable tariff-refund impact of roughly 2 ppts, and diluted EPS includes a +$0.11 benefit. We believe the underlying structural improvement remains positive after stripping out the refund, but its magnitude should be assessed conservatively given the possibility of policy reversals.

  • 3.
    Services and the Installed Base Deepen Ecosystem Stickiness; AI Investment Accelerates

    Services revenue of $30,739 M (+12.1%) set a June-quarter record, and the active installed base reached all-time highs across all major product categories and geographic segments; R&D expense rose 32.3% YoY, and with the all-new Siri AI unveiled at WWDC26 alongside the latest software innovations, Apple's AI roadmap is entering the deployment phase.

  • 4.
    Shares Trade Above the Consensus Target, Implying Elevated Expectations

    We believe the market has already priced in an optimistic scenario: based on the 07/30/2026 closing price of $333.43, the stock trades roughly 3.0% above the Bloomberg ANR 12-month consensus target of $323.27 (Return Potential −3.0%); a Forward P/E of 35.34x also tops the peer group, so further upside requires fundamentals to keep beating expectations.

V. Risk Factors

  1. Tariff & Trade-Policy Uncertainty: This quarter's 50.1% gross margin includes a ~2 ppts favorable tariff-refund impact and diluted EPS includes +$0.11; the company's risk disclosures also cite government policies, trade and other international disputes, and geopolitical tensions. Policy reversals could erode or even reverse this benefit.
  2. Valuation Premium & Inverted Consensus Target: A Forward P/E of 35.34x tops the peer group, and the 07/30/2026 close of $333.43 already exceeds the Bloomberg ANR consensus target of $323.27 by about 3.0%; slowing growth momentum would raise de-rating risk.
  3. AI Competition & New-Product Execution Risk: The real-world experience and monetization of the all-new Siri AI (unveiled at WWDC26) remain to be proven; R&D expense rose 32.3% YoY to $11,729 M, reflecting investment intensity, and weaker-than-expected AI adoption could compress operating leverage.
  4. Legal Proceedings & Regulatory Environment: The company's disclosures flag adverse legal proceedings, government investigations, and complex and changing laws and regulations that could affect its business, reputation, results of operations, financial condition, and stock price.
