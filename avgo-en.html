V. Risk Factors

  1. Macroeconomic Conditions & Industry Cyclicality: Global macroeconomic volatility poses systemic risks; the semiconductor domain faces sweeping structural cyclical shifts as the AI market matures.
  2. Geopolitical Dynamics & Regulatory Pressures: Operating as an international component provider introduces legal and compliance exposures, tariff updates, export restrictions, and international trade gridlocks.
  3. Supply Chain Friction & High Manufacturing Dependency: Utilizing a strict fabless model leaves Broadcom highly exposed to external foundries and outsourcing dependencies, displaying structural vulnerability to single-source allocations for mission-critical parts.
  4. Customer Concentration & Demand Inelasticity: Hyper-scalers and key massive tech corporations compose a high percentage of custom AI accelerator bookings. Sudden rollbacks or delays in their deployment timelines could directly hit top-line financials.
