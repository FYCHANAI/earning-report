I. Our Views

🎯

Core Operational Conclusions

  • 1.
    Cloud Growth Accelerates to +82%; the Full-Stack AI Strategy Enters Its Harvest Phase

    Google Cloud revenue reached $24,768 M, up 82% YoY, with operating margin expanding from 20.7% to 35.6%; backlog rose by more than $50B QoQ to $514B, with slightly over half expected to be recognized over the next 24 months. We believe the first-time recognition of TPU system sales this quarter, together with Gemini Enterprise adoption by nearly 90% of the Fortune 100, shows AI investment converting into a verifiable revenue curve.

  • 2.
    AI-Powered Search Sustains Core Resilience with a 12th Consecutive Quarter of Double-Digit Growth

    Consolidated revenue reached $119,796 M, up 24% YoY (+23% FX-neutral); Search & other +17%, YouTube ads +13%, and subscriptions, platforms & devices +15%. Query volume keeps rising following the integration of AI Overviews and AI Mode; AI Mode surpassed 1 billion MAU, the Gemini App reached 950 million, and World Cup-related YouTube videos drew over 1.7 billion unique viewers.

  • 3.
    Headline EPS Inflated by a One-Off Item; Operating Income Better Reflects Core Momentum

    Diluted EPS of $9.11 (+294%) was driven primarily by $99,031 M of net gains on equity securities (which the company said were mainly driven by unrealized gains); excluding the company-disclosed impact, NTAM mechanically estimates EPS at roughly $2.85 — not a Non-GAAP figure published by Alphabet, and the prior-year quarter also included similar gains, so a direct YoY comparison is not appropriate. We believe operating income growth of +30% and margin expansion of 1.6 ppts to 34.0% better represent the quarter's core performance.

  • 4.
    Capex Cycle Heats Up as the Financing Structure Expands Proactively

    Q2 capex reached $44,924 M (roughly +100%), full-year guidance was raised to $195–205B, and quarterly free cash flow turned negative at -$5,855 M; the company completed $49.6B of equity financing and $20.3B of senior unsecured notes to fund AI infrastructure. We believe capital-allocation pacing and the timing of returns (with the vast majority of TPU agreement revenue to be recognized in 2027) will be the key valuation variables ahead.

IV. Analyst Views

(Ratings and price targets: Bloomberg ANR, as of 07/23/2026) The Bloomberg 12-month consensus price target is $434.51, shown as 58/83 target samples on the screen; separately there are 74 effective ratings — 66 Buys, 8 Holds and 0 Sells — with market consensus leaning toward Buy.

UBS

Neutral
  • AI Momentum Largely Priced In (PT $400): Search & other +17% and Cloud +82% are both strong, but after the sharp rally the stock trades close to the target, with much of the AI upside already reflected in the valuation.
  • Capex and Cash-Flow Pressure to Digest: FY2026 capex guidance was raised to $195–205B and Q2 free cash flow turned negative at $5.9B; third-party compute bridging and Wiz integration costs will also weigh on Cloud margins near term.

Citi

Buy
  • Cloud Acceleration Alongside Resilient Ads (PT $447): Cloud growth jumped from +32% a year ago to +82% with backlog reaching $514B; Search still delivered +17% amid the full rollout of AI Overviews/AI Mode, as the full-stack AI strategy enters its harvest phase.
  • Gemini Deeply Integrated into the Ads Stack: AI Max has surpassed 500,000 advertisers; customers adopting AI-powered campaigns see roughly 15% more conversions at similar ROAS, with retail and finance the largest contributors to Search growth.

Barclays

Overweight
  • External TPU Sales Open a New Growth Curve (PT $425): First-time recognition of TPU system sales delivered to customer data centers, with the vast majority of related revenue to be recognized in 2027; combined with the $514B backlog, medium-term revenue visibility improves markedly.
  • Model Iteration Cadence Accelerating: Gemini 3.6 Flash launches continue and Gemini 4 pre-training is underway; Gemini and Google's first-party model APIs process roughly 22 billion tokens per minute (vs. 16B+ last quarter), with inference demand still supply-constrained.

V. Risk Factors

  1. Capex Cycle & Free-Cash-Flow Pressure: FY2026 capex guidance was raised from $180–190B to $195–205B, with management flagging a significant further increase in 2027; quarterly free cash flow turned negative at -$5,855 M. The company has replenished capital via $49.6B of equity financing, $20.3B of senior unsecured notes and a $40B ATM program (unused as of quarter-end), lifting long-term debt to $98.2B; depreciation and data-center operating costs (e.g., energy) will keep pressuring the income statement.
  2. Near-Term Cloud Margin Pressure: Management stated it will expand the use of third-party compute as a bridging strategy from Q3, and these costs, together with Wiz integration expenses, will pressure Cloud operating margin in the near term; the margin profile of TPU system sales is also not separately disclosed.
  3. High Volatility of Equity Securities Gains: This quarter's $99,031 M of net gains on equity securities (which the company said were mainly driven by unrealized gains) added $6.26 to diluted EPS; the company noted that swings in investment values may significantly increase future OI&E volatility, reducing period-over-period comparability of headline earnings.
  4. Competition, FX and Regulatory Environment: The frontier-model race remains intense and compute allocation involves trade-offs in a supply-constrained environment; management expects a slight FX headwind in Q3 (mainly affecting Search and YouTube ads); part of this quarter's G&A growth also came from charges for certain legal and other matters.
