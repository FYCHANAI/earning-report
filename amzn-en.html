I. Our Views

🎯

Core Operational Conclusions

  • 1.
    AWS Growth Re-Accelerated to +37%, the Fastest in 18 Quarters

    AWS quarterly net sales reached $42,232 M (YoY +37%); the company disclosed an annualized revenue run rate of $169 billion, with the AI business and chips business each surpassing a $25 billion annualized run rate on triple-digit YoY growth. Segment operating income was $16,621 M (+64%) at a 39.4% margin. We believe AWS's return to a high-growth trajectory is the most important operating signal this quarter.

  • 2.
    Net Income +245% Requires Isolating a Non-Operating Gain

    Q2 net income was $62,647 M with diluted EPS of $5.75 (+242%), but this includes $53.4 billion of pre-tax, non-operating other income (primarily from investments in Anthropic), which is not core operating profit. We believe the core business should be assessed on operating income of $27,461 M (+43%) and the 13.7% operating margin (11.4% a year ago).

  • 3.
    Solid Retail & Advertising Momentum, but AI Capex Turned TTM Free Cash Flow Negative

    North America grew +16% and International +15%; advertising services revenue reached $19,809 M (+26%); the company disclosed another Prime delivery-speed record in the first half. TTM operating cash flow (GAAP) rose +33% to $161.4 billion, but TTM net capital expenditures reached $169.0 billion (up $66.1 billion YoY, primarily reflecting AI investments per the company), turning TTM free cash flow (Non-GAAP) to -$7.6 billion.

  • 4.
    Solid Q3 Guidance; Upside Remains to the Consensus Target

    The company guided Q3 2026 net sales of $197.0–202.0 billion (+9%–12%; excluding the impact of Prime Day in both 2025 and 2026, YoY growth would be nearly 400 basis points higher; FX is an approximately 80-basis-point headwind) and operating income of $22.5–26.5 billion (vs. $17.4 billion a year ago). Based on the July 30, 2026 closing price of $235.50, the Bloomberg ANR 12-month consensus target of $316.05 implies approximately +34.2% upside (Return Potential +34.2%). We believe the key watch item is whether AWS growth continues to absorb capex intensity.

V. Risk Factors

  1. Execution & Payback Risk in the AI Capex Cycle: TTM net capital expenditures reached $169.0 billion (up $66.1 billion YoY, primarily reflecting AI investments per the company), turning TTM free cash flow (Non-GAAP) to -$7.6 billion; quarterly depreciation and amortization was $19,988 M versus $15,227 M a year ago. If AWS and AI demand growth slows, rising depreciation could compress margins and extend the period of negative free cash flow.
  2. Volatility of Non-Operating Gains: Q2 net income includes $53.4 billion of pre-tax, non-operating other income (primarily from investments in Anthropic); such investment-related items are inherently uncertain, may reverse in future periods and could materially swing reported net income and EPS — investors should track operating income for the core trend.
  3. Company-Cited Macro & Supply Risks: Guidance lists uncertainties including fluctuations in foreign exchange rates and energy prices, global economic and geopolitical conditions, tariff and trade policies, resource and supply variability (including memory chips), and consumer demand and spending (including recession fears); Q3 2026 guidance already embeds an approximately 80-basis-point FX headwind.
  4. Competition, Regulation & Legal Proceedings: The company's forward-looking statements cite competition, government regulation and taxation, and litigation and government investigations among risk factors; the cloud and AI investment race could also prolong an industry-wide heavy-capex cycle and affect the timing of returns.
